# Class philosophies

Source: *Class Philosophies* (design doc from the game's author). Use this when designing talents, skills and new classes.

Classes fall into four categories:

- **Wardens**: Paladin, Samurai, Berserker *(not in game yet)*
- **Martialists**: Monk, Shinobi *(not in game yet)*, Myrmidon
- **Menders**: Druid, Cleric, Kannagi *(not in game yet)*
- **Sorcerers**: Elementalist, Beastmaster *(not in game yet)*, Necromancer

## Wardens

### Paladin

- **Order:** Order of the Divine Light
- **Patron(s):** Solara, the Radiant Dawn

Trained in the highest order of morality, swordsmanship, shielding, and faith, Paladins are the holy warriors of the Order of the Divine Light. They serve Solara, the Radiant Dawn, a god of justice, purity, and light. Raised in the sanctified halls of the Grand Cathedral, Paladins are instilled with unwavering faith and the strict moral code of their order. They believe in protecting the innocent and vanquishing evil, often seen as the shield of their companions. Through rigorous training in both martial and divine arts, Paladins wield their swords and shields with unmatched precision, calling upon Solara's holy power to smite their foes and protect their allies. They are the embodiment of justice, ready to lay down their lives for the greater good.

> In the light of Solara, we find our strength. In the darkness, we bring forth her dawn.

### Samurai

- **Order:** Amakiri Reiken Style
- **Patron(s):** Arathorn, the God of War

The Samurai of the Amakiri Reiken Style are the paragons of honor, discipline, and martial prowess. Founded by the legendary swordsman Amakiri Reiken, this school emphasizes perfecting the katana, the iconic weapon of the Samurai. Trained from a young age in the art of bushido, Samurai are taught the principles of loyalty, courage, and respect. They master various forms of swordplay, from swift and precise strikes to powerful, sweeping slashes. The Amakiri Reiken Style also teaches its students to harness their inner spirit, allowing them to perform extraordinary feats of agility and strength. Samurai are known for their calm and composed demeanor, entering battle with a clear mind and unwavering focus. Though they may not worship him, they are protected by Arathorn, the God of War, who admires their dedication to martial excellence. They are the defenders of their lords and the enforcers of justice, their blades a symbol of their commitment to their code.

> In every swing of the sword, we honor our ancestors. Through discipline and courage, we uphold the code of the Amakiri Reiken.

### Berserker (not in game yet)

- **Order:** Howl of the War Wolf
- **Patron(s):** Arathorn, the God of War; Fenrisulfr, the Wild Father

Berserkers are the fearsome warriors of the Howl of the War Wolf, worshipping Arathorn and Fenrisulfr. These warriors thrive in the chaos of battle, their bodies becoming vessels of Arathorn’s and Fenrisulfr's fury. Raised in harsh environments and trained in brutal combat techniques, Berserkers learn to harness their rage to fuel their strength. They wield massive weapons with ease, dealing devastating area-of-effect damage while sustaining themselves through lifesteal abilities. The more they fight, the more frenzied they become, entering a berserk state where their power is unmatched. Their high health and relentless assault make them formidable tanks, capable of withstanding immense damage and dishing it out. Berserkers are both feared and revered for their wild, untamed nature and their unyielding loyalty to the Howl of the War Wolf.

> In the rage of battle, we are reborn. Arathorn’s fury flows through us, and in our heart beats Fenrisulfr’s wild desire, unyielding and unstoppable.

## Martialists

### Monk

- **Order:** Circle of the Verdant Heart
- **Patron(s):** Elandria, the Earth Mother; Fenrisulfr, the Wild Father; Nimue, the Spirit Weaver

The Monks of the Circle of the Verdant Heart live in secluded monasteries, where they dedicate their lives to meditation, physical perfection, and the study of nature's mysteries. Guided by the teachings of Elandria, Fenrisulfr, and Nimue, they believe in harmony between body, mind, and the natural world. These ascetics have developed a unique martial arts style that mimics the forms and movements of animals, channeling the spirit of nature through their every strike. Their tranquil demeanor hides the formidable power within, capable of unleashing devastating attacks while remaining at peace with their surroundings. Monks are known for their agility and precision, striking with the grace of a crane and the ferocity of a tiger.

> To master oneself is to master the world. Through the spirit of nature, we find balance and strength.

### Shinobi (not in game yet)

- **Order:** Kagekiri Clan
- **Patron(s):** Tsukuyomi, the Veiled Shadow

The Shinobi of the Kagekiri Clan are the epitome of stealth, agility, and lethal precision. Trained in the hidden village of Kiri no Kage (Fog Shadow), these warriors are masters of the silent arts, capable of moving unseen and striking without warning. The Kagekiri Clan has a storied history, tracing its roots to ancient times, when its ancestors served as the secret protectors of emperors and nobles. Shinobi are taught to use the elements to enhance their combat abilities, employing fire, water, lightning, and shadow in their deadly techniques. They are adept at using a variety of weapons, from kunai and shuriken to ninjato and smoke bombs. The Shinobi's loyalty to their clan is absolute, and they carry out their missions with unwavering dedication, whether it be espionage, assassination, or protection. They are guided by Tsukuyomi, the Veiled Shadow, the god of secrets, shadows, and unseen paths, who blesses them with the power to remain hidden and strike swiftly.

> From the shadows we strike, unseen and unstoppable. The Kagekiri is our family, and in silence, we find our strength.

### Myrmidon

- **Order:** Iron Gauntlet
- **Patron(s):** Arathorn, the God of War

Myrmidons are the elite mercenaries known for their unmatched skill in weapon mastery and combat. Hailing from the renowned Iron Gauntlet guild, they are sought after by kingdoms and armies for their lethal efficiency. Myrmidons are adept in the use of a wide range of weapons, including swords, axes, spears, and more. Their training emphasizes adaptability and precision, allowing them to switch between weapons seamlessly to exploit their enemies' weaknesses. The Iron Gauntlet guild prides itself on its code of honor and professionalism, ensuring that its members uphold the highest standards of mercenary conduct. Myrmidons take great pride in their craft, viewing each battle as an opportunity to further hone their skills. Their reputation as formidable warriors precedes them, and their presence on the battlefield can turn the tide of any conflict. They are protected by Arathorn, the God of War, who respects their prowess and dedication to the art of combat.

> With steel in our hands and honor in our hearts, we forge our destiny on the battlefield. The Iron Gauntlet guides our path.

## Menders

### Druid

- **Order:** Circle of the Verdant Heart
- **Patron(s):** Elandria, the Earth Mother; Fenrisulfr, the Wild Father; Nimue, the Spirit Weaver

Druids are the caretakers of the natural world, revered as the living embodiment of Elandria's will. They can shapeshift into powerful creatures and call upon the elements to heal and protect. Raised in sacred groves and ancient forests, Druids are taught the secrets of nature from a young age. They are capable of transforming into bears for raw strength and resilience or into Alraune to harness healing and control. Druids wield the forces of nature with unmatched skill, summoning plants to ensnare their foes and animals to aid them in battle. Their deep connection to the Earth Mother allows them to heal the wounded and restore balance to the natural world.

> In every leaf, in every beast, we see Elandria’s touch. We are the guardians of her will, the healers of her wounds.

### Cleric

- **Order:** Order of the Divine Light
- **Patron(s):** Solara, the Radiant Dawn

Clerics of the Order of the Divine Light are battle priests who combine their fervent faith in Solara with martial prowess. Clad in mail armor, they stride confidently into battle, healing their allies and smiting their enemies with divine wrath. Raised in the temples alongside the Paladins, Clerics are taught to channel the Radiant Dawn’s power through their prayers and rituals. Their healing touch can mend the gravest of wounds, while their righteous fury can bring terror to the heretics and unholy beings they confront. Clerics act as both the spiritual and physical support of their companions, their presence a beacon of hope and strength in the darkest of times. Their unique ability to fight and heal simultaneously makes them invaluable on the battlefield.

> With every prayer, we heal. With every strike, we deliver justice. In Solara’s name, we fight and protect.

### Kannagi (not in game yet)

- **Order:** Path of the Ancients
- **Patron(s):** The Eternal Ones

The Kannagi are revered as the bridge between the mortal realm and the mysterious Eternal Ones, entities of immense power and wisdom that predate the gods worshipped by other cultures. Trained from a young age in secluded temples, Kannagi are taught to communicate with these ancient spirits through rituals, meditation, and the invocation of arcane symbols. They serve as both spiritual leaders and powerful mages, capable of summoning barriers, healing allies, and unleashing devastating spells. The Eternal Ones grant them the ability to manipulate both mana and stamina, making Kannagi versatile in both offensive and defensive roles. Their presence on the battlefield is marked by an aura of otherworldly power, as they channel the will of the Eternal Ones to protect and empower their comrades.

> Through the whispers of the Eternal Ones, we find our strength. Their ancient wisdom guides our hand and fortifies our spirit.

## Sorcerers

### Elementalist

- **Order:** The Fifth Element
- **Patron(s):** Aetherion, the Omnielemental Spirit

Elementalists are the brilliant but eccentric spellcasters who have dedicated their lives to the study and mastery of the elements. Often considered the "stupid geniuses" of the magical world, they possess an unparalleled understanding of fire, ice, lightning, and earth. Elementalists are known for their unpredictable and often chaotic nature, as their deep immersion in arcane studies leaves little room for conventional social norms. These mages are capable of wielding the raw power of the elements as weapons, using their dexterity and intelligence to deliver devastating attacks. They are members of The Fifth Element, an organization dedicated to discovering the mythical fifth element. They believe that by mastering and converting the four known elements, they can unlock this ultimate power. Guided by Aetherion, the Omnielemental Spirit, they seek to push the boundaries of elemental magic. Elementalists often reside in isolated towers or academies, surrounded by tomes of ancient knowledge and arcane artifacts. Their passion for discovery drives them to constantly push the boundaries of what is possible, making them both revered and feared for their immense power.

> The elements are our allies, chaotic yet powerful. In their embrace, we find our true potential and unleash it upon the world.

### Beastmaster (not in game yet)

- **Order:** Circle of the Verdant Heart
- **Patron(s):** Elandria, the Earth Mother; Fenrisulfr, the Wild Father; Nimue, the Spirit Weaver

Beastmasters are the legendary hunters and tamers of the wild, forming pacts with mythical beasts to harness their power. They are seen as the chosen of Fenrisulfr, the Wild Father, who grants them the ability to channel the essence of these formidable creatures. Beastmasters are known to roam the untamed wilderness, seeking out legendary beasts to forge powerful bonds. Through these bonds, they can call upon the strength of Fenrisulfr, the defense of Behemoth, the speed of Roc, and the ferocity of Manticore. Their connection to these beasts is so profound that they can momentarily channel their abilities through their own bodies, transforming limbs into claws, fangs, or wings during battle. Beastmasters are versatile warriors, revered and feared for their symbiotic relationship with the wild.

> By Fenrisulfr’s will, we are one with the wild. Through the eyes of beasts, we see our true power.

### Necromancer

- **Order:** Cult of the Black Sun
- **Patron(s):** Hel, the Shadow Keeper

The Necromancers of the Cult of the Black Sun are feared and often misunderstood, wielding the dark arts of life and death with a mastery unmatched by any other. Their god, Hel, the Shadow Keeper, is a deity of death, decay, and the secrets of the afterlife. Necromancers are known to delve into forbidden knowledge, peering into the very fabric of existence to command the forces of death. They are capable of summoning undead minions, casting powerful curses, and draining their enemies' life force. Raised in secretive cabals, Necromancers are taught to harness their own life, mana, and stamina to fuel their devastating spells. Their devotion to Hel grants them a unique perspective on the cycle of life and death, making them both revered and feared as they walk the thin line between the living and the dead.

> In the shadow of Hel, we find our power. Life and death are but two sides of the same coin, and we are its masters.
