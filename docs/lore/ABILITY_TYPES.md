# Ability types

Source: *Ability/Skill Types* (design doc from the game's author). The skill and combo systems build toward these.

| Type | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic Abilities** | Fundamental attacks or skills that form the core of a class's combat repertoire. These abilities are quick to execute and have no special requirements. | A sword slash, a quick jab, or a simple spell cast |
| **Advanced Abilities** | More powerful and complex moves that often require specific positioning, timing, or conditions to activate. These abilities offer significant advantages in combat. | A charged strike that deals more damage when hitting from behind, a spell that requires channeling, or a powerful combo finisher |
| **Link Abilities** | These abilities are designed to connect with other abilities to create fluid and dynamic combat sequences. They allow players to chain Basic -> Basic, Basic -> Advanced, and Advanced -> Basic abilities seamlessly. | A quick step that follows up a basic attack with another attack, a spell that enhances the next attack, or a dodge that sets up a counter-strike |
| **Buff Abilities** | Skills that enhance a character's attributes or abilities temporarily, providing strategic advantages in battle. These can boost stats, resistances, or even grant temporary invincibility. | A defensive stance that increases armor, a spell that boosts attack power, or a chant that regenerates health over time |
| **Passive Abilities** | Innate traits or abilities that provide continuous benefits without needing activation. These define the class's unique strengths and style. | Increased movement speed, natural health regeneration, or a permanent damage boost under certain conditions |
| **Ultimate Abilities** | The pinnacle of a class's power, these abilities embody the essence of the class. They are powerful, game-changing moves with high impact but longer cooldowns or significant costs. | A massive area-of-effect spell, an unstoppable berserk mode, or a guardian's shield that absorbs all damage for a short time |
| **Transcendent Abilities** | Exceptionally rare and powerful skills that link advanced abilities to ultimate abilities, creating devastating combinations. These are the epitome of a player's mastery over their class. | A sequence where a powerful attack flows into an ultimate move, amplifying its effects, or a spell that enhances the potency of the ultimate ability |
| **Hidden Abilities** | Secret abilities that players discover through experimentation with different link combos. These add a layer of mystery and encourage exploration of the class's full potential. | An unknown combo that, when performed correctly, triggers a hidden technique or grants a temporary powerful buff |
