# The Creation of Eledoria: A Sacred Scripture

Source: pantheon creation myth (from the game's author), for flavour and concept.

In the beginning, there was only the vast expanse of the cosmos, an infinite void where the primordial being Nyxara, the Eternal Void, existed. Nyxara embodied the essence of creation and destruction, an entity of immeasurable power and knowledge. For eons, Nyxara maintained a delicate balance within its being, a harmony that kept the universe in a state of perfect equilibrium.

But this harmony was disrupted by the emergence of Xalathar, the Everchanging Abyss, a force of pure chaos and entropy. Xalathar encroached upon Nyxara, causing ripples of discord and imbalance.

The balance within Nyxara fractured, and in a cataclysmic event known as the Shattering of Nyxara, the Eternal Void was torn asunder, giving birth to the universe. From the remnants of Nyxara, the world of Eledoria was created.

From Nyxara's essence, the Ancient Ones emerged, embodying the primal forces of creation and destruction. Following them, Aetherion, the Omnielemental Spirit, was born from Nyxara’s heart, representing the unity of elements and the foundation of existence.

Nimue, the Spirit Weaver, emerged from Nyxara’s blood, embodying the flow of life. Elandria, the Earth Mother, formed from Nyxara’s lungs, symbolizing the breath of life and nurturing nature.

From the stomach of Nyxara came Fenrisulfr, the Wild Father, representing primal instincts and sustenance. Solara, the Radiant Dawn, emerged from Nyxara’s eyes, bringing clarity and vision, and casting light upon the world.

Arathorn, the God of War, was born from Nyxara’s hands, embodying strength and protection. Hel, the Shadow Keeper, emerged from Nyxara’s bones, representing the structure and the cycle of life and death.

Finally, Tsukuyomi, the Veiled Shadow, was born from Nyxara’s mind, symbolizing wisdom and secrets.

The Shattering of Nyxara not only created the gods and Eledoria but also set the stage for an eternal struggle between the forces of order and chaos. Though weakened, Xalathar continued to seek to disrupt the balance.

The gods, each born from different aspects of Nyxara, formed alliances and rivalries, shaping the fate of Eledoria. The balance between creation and destruction, order and chaos, became the driving force behind the world's evolution.

Thus, Eledoria was born from the essence of Nyxara, a world of wonder shaped by the divine, a testament to the eternal dance of creation and destruction.
